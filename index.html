Process

How I build Shopify stores that perform

A clear, collaborative process that takes your store from an initial idea to a polished, high-performing Shopify experience built around your brand, customers, and business goals.

  1. Discover

    Understand your brand, audience, goals, and Shopify requirements to establish a strong foundation.

  2. Plan

    Define the structure, user journey, features, and project direction before design begins.

  3. Design

    Create intuitive UI/UX with thoughtful layouts, typography, and conversion-focused experiences.

  4. Develop

    Turn designs into responsive Shopify storefronts with clean, flexible, custom development.

  5. Integrate

    Connect essential apps, tools, and third-party services for a seamless ecommerce experience.

  6. Optimize

    Test and refine performance, responsiveness, usability, and conversion details.

  7. Launch

    Complete final checks, launch your store, and provide support for ongoing improvements.
